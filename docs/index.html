Your training.
Set by set.

Build a routine, log your sets offline, and see how your strength changes over time. An open-source workout tracker for Android and iOS.

Get ZeroBuff ↓

Train without a signal.

ZeroBuff saves sets on your device as you train. If the app closes mid-session, reopen it and pick up where you left off.

You’ll need a connection to edit routines or your schedule and to load past workouts.

  1. Before you go offline

    Sign in and open Today while connected so ZeroBuff can download your scheduled routine.

  2. During your workout

    Start the downloaded routine from Today. Log sets, use the rest timer, and finish the session offline.

  3. When you reconnect

    Completed workouts sync automatically. You can see which workouts are waiting to sync in Analytics.

Plan, train, and review.

  1. Plan your week

    Build routines with your own exercise order, sets, reps, and rest times. Assign routines to weekdays and open Today to see what’s scheduled and how consistently you’ve trained.

  2. Log every set

    Record weight, reps, and optional reps in reserve (RIR). See your previous sets, switch exercises, add or remove sets, and pause the workout when you need a break.

  3. Review your progress

    See a summary when you finish, browse your workout history, and edit past sets. Compare sessions and chart load, reps, volume, and estimated one-rep max by exercise.

  • Exercise catalog

    Search by name, filter by muscle group or equipment, and create custom exercises for your routines.

  • Rest timer and alerts

    Start the timer manually or automatically after a set. Enable notifications for an alert when rest ends. On supported devices, follow the countdown outside the app.

  • Body-weight trends

    Log body weight from Today and review daily measurements or weekly averages in Analytics.

  • Profile and language

    Manage your profile and choose English, Italian, or your device language. The built-in catalog shows Italian exercise names when the app is set to Italian.
