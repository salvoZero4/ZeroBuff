Build a routine, log every lift, and see how your strength changes over time. An open-source workout tracker for iOS and Android.

Get ZeroBuff ↓

From plan to progress.

  1. Plan your week

    Choose exercises from the catalog or create your own. Set the order, sets, reps, and rest times, then assign routines to weekdays.

  2. Log every set

    Record weight, reps, and optional reps in reserve. Compare with your last session, switch exercises as equipment frees up, and let the rest timer tell you when to go again.

  3. Review your progress

    Explore your workout history and track load, reps, volume, and estimated one-rep max by exercise. Log body weight to follow it over time.
